Canton solvency — offline verifier

Check that your balance is inside a venue's published totals. Everything happens in this page: it makes no network requests, so you can save it and run it with your connection off.

What you need

“recomputed here” means this page derived the value from the commitment itself. “publisher says” means the value is signed but cannot be proven by one inclusion proof — a single proof shows your entry is in the tree, not that every other customer is.

Getting the publisher key from the same place you got the report proves nothing. Obtain it independently.
